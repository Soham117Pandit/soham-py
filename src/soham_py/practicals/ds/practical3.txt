Suppose the manufacturer claims that the mean lifetime of a ball bearing is 10000 hours. The auditing team stated that the mean lifetime is less than what is claimed. On the basis of a randomly chosen sample of 50 ball bearings as given in the dataset, at 0.05 significance level, can we reject the claim of the manufacturer? What will be your interpretation if the significance level is made as 0.01? Consider the dataset titled "Hypothesis_csv1.csv".

# Experiment 3: Hypothesis Testing on Ball Bearing Lifetime

# Step 1: Import required libraries
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np
from scipy import stats

# Step 2: Load dataset
data = pd.read_csv("Hypothesis_csv1.csv")

# Step 3: Extract Lifetime column
sample_data = data['Lifetime'].values

# Step 4: Define hypotheses and significance levels
claimed_mean = 10000

alpha_05 = 0.05
alpha_01 = 0.01

# Step 5: Calculate sample statistics
sample_size = len(sample_data)
sample_mean = np.mean(sample_data)
sample_std = np.std(sample_data, ddof=1)

# Step 6: Perform one-sample left-tailed t-test
t_stat, p_value = stats.ttest_1samp(
    sample_data,
    claimed_mean,
    alternative='less'
)

# Step 7: Display results
print("Sample size:", sample_size)
print("Sample mean:", round(sample_mean, 2), "hours")
print("Sample standard deviation:", round(sample_std, 2), "hours")

print("T-statistic:", round(t_stat, 4))
print("P-value:", format(p_value, '.6f'))

# Step 8: Test at 5% significance level
print("\nAt significance level 0.05:")

if p_value < alpha_05:
    print("Reject H0: Mean lifetime is less than 10000 hours.")
else:
    print("Fail to reject H0.")

# Step 9: Test at 1% significance level
print("\nAt significance level 0.01:")

if p_value < alpha_01:
    print("Reject H0: Strong evidence that mean lifetime is less than 10000 hours.")
else:
    print("Fail to reject H0.")

# Step 10: Calculate critical t-values
df = sample_size - 1

t_crit_05 = stats.t.ppf(alpha_05, df)
t_crit_01 = stats.t.ppf(alpha_01, df)

print("\nCritical t-values:")
print("At 0.05:", round(t_crit_05, 4))
print("At 0.01:", round(t_crit_01, 4))
